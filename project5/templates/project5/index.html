Project Overview

This advanced AI project demonstrates the power of Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF), a technique used in training modern AI systems like ChatGPT. Our mouse agent learns to navigate a grid world through three sophisticated phases:

Phase 1: REINFORCE

Initial policy learning using the REINFORCE algorithm to collect rewards.

Phase 2: Human Feedback

Bradley-Terry model learns from human preferences between trajectories.

Phase 3: Policy Refinement

Final policy optimization using KL divergence penalty for stability.

About RLHF Technology

Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF) is an AI training technique that combines:

  • Traditional RL: Learning from environmental rewards
  • Human Preferences: Learning from human judgments about behavior quality
  • Preference Modeling: Using Bradley-Terry models to understand human preferences

This technique is used in training modern AI systems like ChatGPT, Claude, and other advanced language models to better align with human values and preferences.

In this project: The mouse learns to prefer regular cheese over organic cheese through human feedback, even though both provide equal environmental rewards!
