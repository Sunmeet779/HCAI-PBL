Task 1: Supervised Text Classification

Classifier Description

This project uses a two-step classifier for sentiment analysis:

  • Text Representation (Encoding): Converts text into numerical vectors using different methods:
    • TF-IDF: Weighs words by importance based on term frequency and inverse document frequency.
    • Bag of Words: Counts word occurrences without considering order or importance.
    • Word2Vec: Learns word embeddings that capture semantic similarity by training on the corpus.
    • LDA (Latent Dirichlet Allocation): Topic modeling method that represents documents as mixtures of topics.
  • Classification Module: Logistic Regression classifier trained on the encoded vectors to predict sentiment.
