Accuracy vs. Interpretability Tradeoff

Low Alpha (Complex Trees)

  • Higher accuracy as the model can capture more complex patterns
  • Harder to interpret with many decision nodes
  • Higher risk of overfitting to the training data

High Alpha (Simple Trees)

  • Potentially lower accuracy as the model is simpler
  • Easier to interpret with fewer decision nodes
  • Lower risk of overfitting to the training data
